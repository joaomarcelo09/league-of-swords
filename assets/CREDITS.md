# Credits

League of Swords is a non-commercial hobby project and will not be sold.

## Art and audio

The sprite and tile assets committed to the repository (`assets/characters/placeholder/`, `assets/tiles/placeholder_tiles.png`) are original placeholder art, procedurally generated by `tools/gen_placeholder_sprites.py`.

Third-party art (LPC sprites and tiles, game-icons.net icons) is **not committed**. It is listed with pinned URLs and sha256 hashes in `data/art_manifest.json`, downloaded by `tools/fetch_art.sh` into the gitignored folders below, and credited in the table at the end of this file. Without it the game keeps using the procedural placeholders. No third-party audio is included yet.
