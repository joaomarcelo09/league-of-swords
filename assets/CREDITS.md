# Credits

League of Swords is a non-commercial hobby project and will not be sold.

## Art and audio

All sprite and visual assets currently in the repository (`assets/characters/placeholder/`) are original placeholder art, procedurally generated by `tools/gen_placeholder_sprites.py`. No third-party artwork or audio is included yet.
